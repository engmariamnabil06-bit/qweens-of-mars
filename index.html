THE MOON

The Moon Car

Lunar Roving Vehicle

In 1971, astronauts drove an electric car on the Moon for the first time. It helped them travel farther, collect Moon rocks, and explore more places. The car was left on the Moon after the mission.

THE MOON

The Landing Machine

Lunar Module Descent Stage

The Lunar Module carried astronauts down to the Moon. After the astronauts returned to their spacecraft, the lower part stayed behind. It became part of the Moon's history.

The Silent Scientists

ALSEP Experiments

Astronauts placed scientific instruments on the Moon. These machines studied moonquakes, the lunar environment, and the Moon's interior. They sent important information back to Earth.

MARS

Opportunity's Landing

Mars Landing Airbags

In 2004, airbags helped the Opportunity rover land safely on Mars. They protected the rover while it bounced on the surface. Their marks remained on Mars after landing.

NASA Official Source ↗
MARS

Perseverance's Journey

Landing System

In 2021, NASA's Perseverance rover landed safely on Mars. Its parachute and protective backshell helped it reach the surface. These parts were left behind on Mars, while the rover continued its mission.
