Some Things Stay Behind

Every space mission leaves something behind. These objects may seem forgotten, but each one carries a remarkable story of human exploration.

01
THE MOON

The Lunar Rovers

During Apollo 15, 16, and 17, astronauts used electric vehicles to explore the Moon. After completing their missions, the three Lunar Roving Vehicles were left behind on the lunar surface.

THE MOON

The Lunar Module

The Lunar Module helped astronauts land on the Moon and return to lunar orbit. Its lower half, called the descent stage, remained on the lunar surface after the astronauts left for their journey back to Earth.

04
MARS

Opportunity's Landing Legacy

When Opportunity landed on Mars in 2004, its landing system helped it reach the surface. Its lander, parachute, and heat shield remained behind. Years later, NASA's HiRISE camera photographed these historic remains from orbit.

NASA Source ↗
05
MARS

Perseverance's Landing Debris

In February 2021, Perseverance landed in Jezero Crater. Its parachute, backshell, heat shield, and descent stage separated during landing and remained on the Martian surface.
